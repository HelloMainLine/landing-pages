# Brand Brief

## Purpose
Convert visitors into leads through a clean, professional landing page that emphasizes trust and clarity over flash.

## Tone
Confident, understated, premium. Think Apple product pages meets high-end architecture firm.

## Audience
Decision-makers (CEOs, founders, directors) who value substance over hype. They're overwhelmed by flashy marketing sites.

## Differentiation
- Zero gradients. Zero neon. Zero "disrupt" language.
- Generous whitespace as the primary design element.
- Typography and spacing create all visual interest.
- Color palette is strictly monochromatic with ONE muted accent for CTAs only.

## Design Direction: Premium Light
- White/off-white backgrounds with subtle grey layering
- Large, confident typography (Space Grotesk for headings, Inter for body)
- High contrast text on light backgrounds
- Subtle borders and shadows for depth
- Minimal imagery — product screenshots or clean illustrations only

## Color Palette (Neutral)
- Primary: #18181b (near-black text)
- Secondary: #71717a (muted grey text)
- Accent: #3b82f6 (one blue for CTAs only)
- Background: #ffffff (pure white)
- Surface: #fafafa (subtle card backgrounds)
- Border: #e4e4e7 (light grey borders)

## Typography
- Headings: Space Grotesk, 48-72px, tight tracking
- Body: Inter, 16-18px, 1.6 line height
- Code/Technical: JetBrains Mono

## Spacing Scale
- Section padding: 128px vertical
- Content max-width: 1152px
- Card padding: 32px
- Element gaps: 16px, 24px, 32px

## Anti-Patterns (DO NOT USE)
- Dark mode backgrounds
- Gradient text or backgrounds
- Animated particle effects
- "Welcome to" or "About Us" language
- Generic stock photos
- More than one accent color
- Rounded corners > 12px
- Box shadows > 2 layers
