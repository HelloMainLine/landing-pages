Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you don't see yours below, just reach out.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract? +
No. All our memberships are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Cancel anytime with 30 days notice. We believe you should stay because you want to, not because you're locked in.
What's included in the free trial? +
Your 7-day free trial includes full gym access, two complimentary group classes, and a one-on-one fitness assessment with a coach. No credit card required to start.
How close is the gym to the Loop? +
We're located at 215 S. Wabash Ave, right in the heart of the Loop. It's a 3-minute walk from the Washington/Wabash CTA station (Red, Brown, Orange, Pink, Green lines) and directly accessible from the Pedway.
Are the classes suitable for beginners? +
Absolutely. Every class has modifications for all fitness levels. Our coaches will show you the basics during your free orientation, and you'll never feel out of place. We've had members who'd never stepped foot in a gym transform their lives here.
What are your operating hours? +
Starter members have access from 6am to 10pm daily. Unlimited and Elite members enjoy 24/7 access with keycard entry. Group class schedules vary by day — check our class timetable online for the latest schedule.
Can I freeze or pause my membership? +
Yes. You can freeze your membership for up to 30 days per year at no charge. Just let your coach know through the app or at the front desk. Medical freezes are available with documentation and can extend beyond 30 days.
