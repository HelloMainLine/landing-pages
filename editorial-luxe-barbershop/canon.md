# Canon — The Rebel Chair

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** The Rebel Chair
- **Tagline:** Where Craft Meets Rebellion
- **Tone:** Editorial, confident, slightly irreverent — "craft not SaaS"
- **Voice:** Direct, honest, no-nonsense barbershop owner who takes pride in every cut

## Color Tokens
- **Canvas:** #FAFAF7 (ivory, warm white)
- **Ink:** #1A1A1A (near-black for text and borders)
- **Ink-Light:** #3D3D3D (secondary text)
- **Brass:** #B18A4A (accent — large text ≥18px only, 3.6:1 contrast on ivory)
- **Brass-Dark:** #8A6A32 (hover states, borders)
- **Border:** #1A1A1A (1px solid ink borders)
- **Surface:** #F5F4F0 (card backgrounds, slightly darker than canvas)
- **Muted:** #6B6B6B (tertiary text, captions)

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Fraunces (Google Fonts), weight 400/600/700
- **Body:** Work Sans (Google Fonts), weight 400/500
- **Code/Labels:** Work Sans, weight 500, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em

## Spacing Scale
- Section padding: 6rem top/bottom (desktop), 4rem (mobile)
- Card padding: 2.5rem
- Grid gap: 2rem
- Heading margins: 1rem bottom
- Paragraph margins: 1.5rem bottom

## Design Rules
- **Zero radius** — all corners are 0px (sharp, editorial, premium)
- **No shadows** — depth via 1px ink borders and contrast only
- **Brass accent** — restricted to text ≥18px and decorative lines/borders
- **Fraunces serif** for all headlines (editorial, publication-grade)
- **Work Sans** for body text (clean, modern, readable)
- **Progressive enhancement** — `.js-ready` gate on all animations
