# Nail Salon Website — Industry Research

## 1. Hero Design Patterns
- **Full-screen hero** with high-quality salon interior image or showcase nail art close-ups
- **Carousel hero** (3-5 slides) cycling through: exterior/interior, services collage, featured nail art, team, seasonal promo
- **Overlay text**: salon name, tagline ("Where Art Meets Elegance"), CTA button ("Book Appointment" or "View Services")
- **Video background** becoming standard — showing nail artistry in slow motion
- **Mobile**: simplified single-image hero, sticky CTA button always visible

## 2. Service Menu Structures
- **Category grid**: Manicure, Pedicure, Gel, Acrylic, Dip Powder, Nail Art, Waxing, Eyelash Extensions
- **Each service card**: service name, description (2-3 lines), price, duration, "Book Now" button
- **Add-on/upgrade section**: nail art per finger, gel top coat, paraffin wax, designs
- **Modal/drawer** for full service detail instead of separate pages
- **Pricing table** approach common: service × duration × price columns

## 3. Booking Flow Conventions
- **Embedded booking widget** (Vagaro, Booksy, Schedulicity, or custom) — step 1: select service → step 2: select staff → step 3: pick date/time → step 4: enter info → confirm
- **Real-time availability** with color-coded time slots (green=available, gray=booked)
- **Staff selection**: show photos + specialties + experience years
- **Booking confirmation**: email + SMS text confirmation with auto-reminder
- **Online booking CTA** in header (sticky or prominent), hero section, and footer
- **Walk-in option** for shops that accept them with estimated wait time

## 4. Pricing Display Norms
- **Price range format**: "$25 - $45" or "Starting at $35" for variable-price services
- **Tiered pricing**: Basic/Deluxe/Premium for each service category
- **Add-on pricing** as a separate column or "upgrade" chip
- **Seasonal promotions** or new client specials highlighted with badge
- **No hidden fees** — clean upfront pricing is trust signal

## 5. Gallery / Portfolio Expectations
- **High-resolution nail art gallery** — grid or masonry layout
- **Category filters**: "All", "Gel Art", "3D Designs", "Ombre", "French Tips", "Seasonal"
- **Before/after** for transformations (nail repair, extensions)
- **Salon interior gallery** to showcase ambiance and cleanliness
- **Instagram feed integration** or "Follow us on Instagram" section
- **Client photo submission** with consent (growing trend)
- **Zoom on hover/tap** for detail on nail art close-ups

## 6. Social Proof Patterns
- **Google Reviews widget** or aggregate star rating in hero/footer
- **Testimonial carousel** with client photo, name, service received, date
- **Review count** alongside star rating ("★ 4.8 (127 reviews)")
- **Before/after + mini review** combo cards
- **Awards and certifications** displayed as badges
- **Featured in** (local magazine features, TV segments, influencer mentions)

## 7. Accessibility Considerations
- **Contrast ratios**: ensure pink/muted palettes pass WCAG AA (4.5:1 for text)
- **Touch targets**: minimum 44×44px for all interactive elements
- **Form labels**: proper `<label>` association on booking forms
- **Alt text** on all gallery images describing nail art and colors
- **Keyboard navigation** through service menus and booking flow
- **Screen reader announcements** for booking confirmation
- **Font size**: minimum 16px for body text to prevent iOS zoom

## 8. SEO for Local Service Businesses
- **Local business schema** (JSON-LD): name, address, phone, hours, service area, service types
- **Google Business Profile** integration with booking link
- **Location page** if multi-location: address, map, specific phone, hours per location
- **Service page SEO**: individual pages or anchors for each major service (manicure, pedicure, gel nails)
- **NAP consistency**: name, address, phone on every page (header/footer)
- **Reviews schema**: aggregateRating markup for star display in SERPs
- **FAQ schema** for common questions (how long does gel last? pricing? walk-ins?)
- **Local keywords**: "[city] nail salon", "[city] manicure", "[neighborhood] nail art"
- **Blog/content**: nail care tips, seasonal nail art trends, aftercare guides
- **Image alt text** with descriptive keywords for gallery images

## 9. Standard Site Sections
1. Header: logo, nav (Services, Gallery, About, Pricing, Book Now CTA)
2. Hero
3. Services & Pricing (often combined)
4. Gallery / Portfolio
5. About Us / Team
6. Testimonials / Reviews
7. Location & Hours
8. Booking Widget / Contact
9. Footer: NAP, social links, hours, quick links, booking CTA

## 10. Competitive Differentiators
- **Virtual nail try-on** (AR) — emerging but still rare
- **Online gift cards** with digital delivery
- **Membership/subscription** (monthly gel refills for flat rate)
- **Loyalty program** with points tracking in booking portal
- **Real-time SMS confirmation** with 2-way reply
- **Client portal** to view past services and re-book favorite services
