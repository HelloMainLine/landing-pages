# Canon — CLIP. (Neo-Brutalist Barbershop)

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** CLIP.
- **Tagline:** Cut Different.
- **Tone:** Bold, irreverent, unapologetic — "brutalist but makes you want to book"
- **Voice:** Direct, punchy, slightly sarcastic but warm underneath

## Color Tokens
- **Bg:** #FFFDF7 (warm off-white)
- **Black:** #0D0D0D (outlines, headings)
- **Yellow:** #FFE600 (primary accent)
- **Pink:** #FF6B9D (secondary accent)
- **Cyan:** #00D4AA (tertiary accent)
- **Lavender:** #E8D5F5 (background accent)
- **Cream:** #FFF3D6 (card backgrounds, alternating)
- **Grid:** #E0DDD5 (subtle grid lines)

## Typography
- **Headlines:** Space Grotesk (Google Fonts), weight 400/500/600/700
- **Body:** Space Mono (Google Fonts) — monospace for that raw, utilitarian feel
- **Labels:** Space Mono, weight 500, uppercase

## Spacing Scale
- Section padding: 5rem top/bottom (desktop), 3.5rem (mobile)
- Card padding: 2rem
- Grid gap: 1.5rem
- Outline thickness: 3px solid #0D0D0D
- Hard shadow: 6px 6px 0px #0D0D0D (solid offset, no blur)

## Design Rules
- **3px black outlines** on everything interactive (buttons, cards, inputs)
- **Hard shadows** (6px solid offset, no blur) on cards and buttons
- **Raw saturated colors** — no gradients, no softness
- **Visible grid lines** as decorative elements
- **Sticker-style badges** with rotation for visual interest
- **Asymmetric layouts** — deliberately off-center where it works
- **Monospace body text** — Space Mono throughout
- **No border-radius** — everything is square

## Section Order (Conversion-Optimized)
1. Hero (asymmetric split)
2. Marquee (scrolling text band)
3. Services (card grid with hard shadows)
4. How It Works (numbered steps with bold colors)
5. Stats (high-contrast block)
6. Testimonials (sticker-style cards)
7. Pricing (3-column with bold accent)
8. FAQ (accordion)
9. Bottom CTA (full-width color block)
10. Footer
