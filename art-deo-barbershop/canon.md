# Canon — The Gilded Razor (Art Deco Barbershop)

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** The Gilded Razor
- **Tagline:** Luxury Redefined
- **Tone:** Opulent, refined, timeless — "1920s grandeur meets modern precision"
- **Voice:** Elegant, confident, sophisticated without being pretentious

## Color Tokens
- **Black:** #0A0A0A (deep background)
- **Gold:** #D4AF37 (primary accent, all decorative elements)
- **Gold-Light:** #F0D060 (highlights, hover states)
- **Cream:** #F5E6C8 (text on dark, card surfaces)
- **Cream-Dark:** #D4C4A0 (secondary text, borders)
- **Green:** #1A3A2A (secondary accent, subtle backgrounds)
- **Green-Light:** #2A5A3A (hover states)

## Typography
- **Display Headlines:** Cormorant Garamond (Google Fonts), weight 600/700
- **Body Headlines:** Cormorant (Google Fonts), weight 600
- **Body Text:** Cormorant (Google Fonts), weight 400/500
- **Labels/Captions:** Cormorant, weight 600, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.15em

## Spacing Scale
- Section padding: 6rem top/bottom (desktop), 4rem (mobile)
- Card padding: 2.5rem
- Grid gap: 2rem
- Ornamental border thickness: 2px gold

## Design Rules
- **Symmetrical layouts** — Art Deco is about balance and geometry
- **Geometric patterns** — diamond/herringbone SVG backgrounds, sunburst dividers
- **Gold accents** — ornamental borders, decorative lines, sunburst icons
- **Deep black backgrounds** — everything sits on #0A0A0A
- **Cream text** — high contrast on dark
- **No border-radius** — sharp, precise geometry
- **Ornamental dividers** — horizontal gold lines with diamond centers
- **Sunburst motif** — decorative section dividers

## Section Order (Conversion-Optimized)
1. Hero (symmetric, centered, ornamental)
2. Ornamental Divider
3. Services (diamond grid)
4. About / Heritage
5. Stats (gold band)
6. Testimonials (elegant cards)
7. Pricing (gold-bordered tiers)
8. FAQ (accordion)
9. Bottom CTA (ornamental frame)
10. Footer
