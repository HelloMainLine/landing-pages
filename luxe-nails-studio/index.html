FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend booking 1-2 weeks in advance for weekends, and 3-5 days for weekday appointments. Walk-ins are welcome subject to availability, but we can't guarantee your preferred time slot.
What products do you use? +
We use only premium, non-toxic products from brands like OPI, CND, and Essie. All our gels are formulated to be 9-free (no formaldehyde, toluene, DBP, and other harmful chemicals). We're committed to safe, healthy nail care.
How long does a gel manicure last? +
With proper care, our gel manicures typically last 2-3 weeks without chipping. We recommend applying cuticle oil daily and wearing gloves for heavy chores to extend the life of your manicure.
Can I bring reference photos for nail art? +
Absolutely! We love when clients bring inspiration. Please share your reference photos during booking so our artist can prepare. Complex designs may require an additional time slot — we'll let you know when you book.
Do you offer gift cards? +
Yes! We offer digital and physical gift cards in any denomination. They make a perfect gift for birthdays, holidays, or "just because." Purchase them online or at our studio.
What's your cancellation policy? +
We ask for at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows will be charged 50% of the appointment value. We understand life happens — just give us a call!
